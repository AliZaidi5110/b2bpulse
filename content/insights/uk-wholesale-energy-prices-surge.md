## Market Overview

UK wholesale electricity prices surged 18% week-on-week, driven by a combination of reduced wind generation, elevated gas prices, and tightening supply margins ahead of the summer maintenance season.

Day-ahead baseload power reached **£142.80/MWh** on Tuesday — the highest level since December 2024. Gas contracts followed suit, with NBP front-month rising 12% to 82p/therm.

## Key Drivers

### Renewable Output Decline

Offshore wind generation averaged just 4.2 GW over the past week, well below the seasonal norm of 6.8 GW. This forced greater reliance on gas-fired generation and imports via interconnectors.

### Storage Concerns

European gas storage facilities are currently at 58% capacity — approximately 8 percentage points below the five-year average for late June. This structural deficit continues to support premium pricing.

### Demand Signals

Industrial demand remains resilient, with manufacturing PMI readings holding above 50 for the third consecutive month. Data centre expansion is adding incremental baseload requirements across the grid.

## Outlook

Pulse analysts expect prices to remain elevated through Q3 2025, with potential relief from increased LNG arrivals in September. Procurement teams should consider locking in forward contracts for Q4 exposure.
