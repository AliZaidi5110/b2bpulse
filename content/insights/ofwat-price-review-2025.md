## Regulatory Summary

Ofwat has published its final determinations for the 2025–26 price review (PR24), setting out allowed returns, performance commitments, and investment programmes for England and Wales water companies.

## Business Customer Impact

For non-household customers, the key implications include:

- **Average tariff increase of 3.2%** above inflation over the AMP8 period
- Enhanced **leakage reduction targets** that may affect supply reliability metrics
- New **environmental performance standards** driving infrastructure investment

## Regional Variations

Tariff changes will vary significantly by water company region:

| Region | Expected Change | Key Factor |
|--------|----------------|------------|
| Thames Water | +4.1% | Infrastructure backlog |
| United Utilities | +2.8% | Efficiency gains |
| Severn Trent | +3.0% | Environmental investment |
| Anglian Water | +3.5% | Drought resilience |
