## Background

The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) transitions from its transitional reporting phase to full implementation in January 2026. UK energy-intensive industries exporting to the EU face new carbon cost obligations.

## Affected Sectors

CBAM initially covers:

- **Cement and clinker**
- **Iron and steel**
- **Aluminium**
- **Fertilisers**
- **Electricity**
- **Hydrogen**

## Compliance Requirements

From January 2026, importers into the EU must:

1. Purchase CBAM certificates corresponding to embedded emissions
2. Submit quarterly reports with verified carbon data
3. Maintain supply chain emissions documentation for 5 years

## Cost Implications

Corelynk modelling suggests average carbon cost pass-through of **€45–€85 per tonne CO₂** for affected products, depending on production methods and grid carbon intensity.

## Preparing Your Business

- Conduct embedded emissions audits across EU-facing product lines
- Evaluate low-carbon production alternatives
- Engage with EU customers on cost-sharing mechanisms
- Monitor UK government CBAM response proposals
