## Market Snapshot

The UK B2B payments landscape is undergoing rapid transformation. Real-time settlement volumes reached **£4.2 billion** in May 2025, representing a 34% increase compared to the same period last year.

## Adoption Drivers

### Open Banking Maturity

With over 10 million active open banking users in the UK, B2B payment initiation services are now mainstream. Key platforms report average transaction times under 15 seconds.

### ERP Integration

Enterprise resource planning systems from SAP, Oracle, and Microsoft now offer native real-time payment rails, removing the traditional barrier of batch processing.

### Cost Reduction

Real-time payments eliminate float costs and reduce reconciliation overhead. Early adopters report **12–18% reduction** in accounts payable processing costs.

## Competitive Landscape

Fintech challengers continue to gain share:

- **Revolut Business** — 28% SME market penetration
- **Tide** — Strong in micro-business segment
- **Wise Business** — Leading cross-border B2B flows

## Corelynk Recommendation

Treasury teams should evaluate their payment stack against real-time capabilities. Legacy batch systems increasingly represent both cost and competitive disadvantage.
