## Regulatory Change

OFGEM has published consultation outcomes for enhanced business energy market reporting, effective from October 2025. The new framework introduces mandatory quarterly disclosures for all licensed suppliers serving non-domestic customers.

## New Requirements

Suppliers must now publish:

1. **Granular tariff breakdowns** by consumption band and contract type
2. **Customer service metrics** including complaint resolution times
3. **Renewable energy sourcing** percentages by tariff
4. **Exit fee structures** and auto-rollover terms
