## Executive Summary

Pulse's water risk modelling indicates elevated scarcity probability for South East England business customers over the next 18 months. This analysis combines climate projections, reservoir levels, and consumption trend data.

## Risk Indicators

Current risk signals include:

- **Reservoir levels** at 72% — 11 points below the 10-year June average
- **Rainfall deficit** of 18% across the South East catchment area
- **Population growth** adding 2.3% annual demand pressure
- **Agricultural abstraction** competing for limited summer supplies

## Business Impact Scenarios

| Scenario | Probability | Tariff Impact | Supply Risk |
|----------|------------|---------------|-------------|
| Moderate drought | 45% | +5–8% | Low |
| Severe drought | 20% | +12–18% | Medium |
| Emergency measures | 5% | +20%+ | High |

## Mitigation Strategies

Business customers should consider:

1. **Consumption auditing** to identify reduction opportunities
2. **Rainwater harvesting** for non-potable applications
3. **Alternative supply arrangements** including borehole feasibility
4. **Insurance review** for business interruption coverage
